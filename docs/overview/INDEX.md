# Overview Documentation

This category explains what Tracekeeper is, why it exists, and what remains
outside its durable product scope.

## Belongs Here

- Product identity, purpose, roles, terminology, scope, and long-term non-goals
- Project-level success criteria that remain stable across releases

## Does Not Belong Here

- User workflows and feature behavior
- Runtime topology, technology choices, implementation status, or delivery plans
